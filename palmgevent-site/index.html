How we work

Tour → Rider → Show

Tour schedule

Venue dates follow the artist's tour schedule. We find the stadium or arena date that fits the routing.

Production is planned around the artist's technical rider: stage, sound, light and backstage.

Public, ticketed concerts in arenas and stadiums, from doors open to the last song.

Istanbul is between two continents. Every tour passes through.

Palm G Event is an event organization company based in Istanbul. We work on public, ticketed concerts in arenas and stadiums. Production is planned around each artist's technical rider, and venue dates follow the artist's tour schedule.
